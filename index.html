How To Play

1. Flip a card

Click a card to see the symbol underneath.

2. Remember it

Try to remember where you saw that symbol.

3. Find the match

Click another card and try to find its matching symbol.

4. Finish the board

Match everything while using as little time and as few moves as possible.

About 5 × 5 and 7 × 7

Those boards have an odd number of spaces, so they contain one special Wild card.
